# Account Brief

Format: **selfie + text + CTA** single stills. Layout, avatar, boards, posts,
and commands live in `README.md`. This file is the audience and voice memory.

## Account Basics

- Account name: antigpt (Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/antigpt.me/)
- Niche: Student life and study pain. Exams, essays, all-nighters, AI-detector
  anxiety, the gap between how studying looks and how it feels.
- Target audience: High school and college students who study with a laptop and
  AI tools open, care about grades and how their study setup looks, and save
  relatable posts. Secondary: grad students writing long papers.
- Platform priority: Instagram first (confirmed channel), TikTok second.
- Primary performance goal: Saves, comments, and follows first; profile visits
  and clicks to antigpt.me second.
- Product relationship: AntiGPT (antigpt.me) shows up only in the CTA line, in
  the caption or as the on-image close. See `../../product/antigpt-brief.md`
  and `../../product/antigpt-claim-bank.md`.
- Hook pattern: one still of the locked girl in a study scene plus one
  first-person line. Pattern memory in
  `../../references/hook-ideas/cards/2026-09-08-bellajobtips-crying-girl-single.md`.

## Audience Understanding

- What the audience wants: Study less and remember more. A method that works the
  night before the exam. Essays that get done faster and still sound like them.
  Proof that other people feel the same way about school.
- What the audience struggles with: Rereading notes and forgetting everything.
  Starting essays. Turning an AI draft into something they can actually submit.
  Getting flagged by Turnitin or GPTZero, sometimes on writing they did
  themselves. Procrastination dressed up as "organizing."
- What they already believe: Everyone uses ChatGPT for school. Detectors are
  unfair and inconsistent. Highlighting and rereading are not real studying,
  even if they still do it. Aesthetic study setups are motivating.
- What they are tired of hearing: "Just start earlier." "Don't use AI." "Make a
  study schedule." Generic productivity advice with no method behind it.
  Influencers with Ivy credentials telling them to wake up at 5 a.m.
- What would make them save/share/comment: A line that names a private study
  feeling they thought was only theirs. A WISH line that implies a fix without
  lecturing. Anything that shortens the time between "I have to study" and "I
  am done."
- What would make the account feel fake: Guru tone. Fake credentials.
  Motivational quotes. Selling in the hook line. Claiming a method works for
  everyone. Stock-photo studying. Overpromising on detectors ("100%
  undetectable").

## Account Point Of View

- Core belief: Study smarter with systems, not longer with willpower. Using AI
  for school is normal; sounding like a bot is the problem.
- Enemy or tension: Wasted effort. Rereading, passive highlighting, generic
  advice, and robotic AI drafts that get flagged.
- Promise: Every post names one real student feeling or one thing you wish you
  knew. The CTA gives you the tool that makes AI-assisted writing sound human.
- Emotional tone: Calm, specific, slightly dry, peer-to-peer. A smart classmate
  posting from the library, not a coach. First person on the still.
- What the account should never say: "Grind." "Lock in" as advice. "Just
  believe in yourself." "Guaranteed A." "Never get caught." Anything that
  frames students as cheaters or the account as anti-education.

## Copy Rules For The Still

- One idea per still. Two to three short lines, centered, white text with a
  black outline (`tools/fal/caption.py` defaults).
- First person. "Things I WISH I knew..." / "I studied the right way and
  still..." / "nobody tells you...". No lists, no numbered tips on the image.
- The line should read like something posted by accident, not a headline.
- Run Stop Slop (`references/skills/stop-slop/SKILL.md`) on every candidate
  line before showing it.
- Paste candidate lines in chat and wait for approval before rendering.

## Product Mention Rules

- Default visibility: CTA line only (caption, or on-image close under the hook
  line). Never in the hook line.
- Best native placement: "you drafted it, now make it sound like you" framing
  after a writing or essay pain line. Memorization or exam pain lines use a
  standalone closing CTA.
- Claims approved: See `../../product/antigpt-claim-bank.md`. Direct "bypass
  Turnitin / bypass AI detectors" wording is approved by the user.
- Claims flagged: "100% undetectable", pass-rate percentages, user counts,
  named-school testimonials, free-trial framing (it requires a card).

## Visual Rules

- Only the locked avatar (`avatar/asian-girl-avatar.jpg`) appears on posted stills.
- User naming (2026-09-13): call her **Asian girl avatar** and put generated
  candidates in `outputs/`. Swaps keep the board's exact pose and expression;
  change the person's identity, preserving gaze, head angle, and hand placement.
- Study scenes: library, cafe, dorm, empty classroom, late-night desk. Real
  clutter (coffee, laptop, iPad, headphones) over staged aesthetics.
- Keep the amateur phone-photo feel. Slightly imperfect beats polished.
- Rotate scenes so consecutive posts do not share the same board.
