## Pattern Notes (from indexed references)

- Study-aesthetic slideshows lead with a labeled method, not a story. Method
  name first, score or verdict second, one-line reason third.
- Rankings and matchers outperform flat lists because they create comparison
  and comment debate ("mind maps 4/10" invites disagreement).
- Authority framing (Harvard, Oxford, neuroscience) is the dominant trust
  device in the niche. This account cannot borrow it honestly; replace with
  specificity and mechanism ("why rereading feels productive and does
  nothing").
- Closers are "save this for later" or "follow for more". For antigpt the
  closer is the AntiGPT slide; test whether a save prompt fits on the same
  slide or the one before.
- Visuals: candid desk photos, notebooks, flashcards, laptops, warm natural
  light, high-contrast label tags. Matches the user's "study aesthetic" ask.
