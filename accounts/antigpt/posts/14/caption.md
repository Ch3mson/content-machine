## Posting title

3 GPT prompts to cut your word count

## Posting description

over the word limit? give GPT one editing task at a time so you can see what it wants to cut.

start with: "highlight repeated ideas in this draft and show where they appear." decide which version explains the point best. then paste a long paragraph and ask for a shorter version that keeps the meaning, qualifications, evidence and citations.

for the final pass, include the essay question and ask which sentences contribute the least to your answer. read the suggested cuts before accepting them, especially anything that limits a claim or explains a source. check the word count in your document editor.

save these prompts for the last edit. AntiGPT at antigpt.me rewrites AI text to sound human and helps bypass AI detectors when you want a further wording pass.
