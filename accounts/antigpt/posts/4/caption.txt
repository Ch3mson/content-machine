TITLE
3 AI shortcuts for essay research

DESCRIPTION
save these before you open 20 tabs for your next essay 📚

ask GPT for keywords + related terms to use in your searches. when you find a useful paper, upload the PDF and ask for its main argument, evidence and relevant page numbers so you can check the summary.

then paste your source notes into GPT and ask it to group them by theme. use those groups to plan your body paragraphs, with the source links beside each point.

save this for your next research session + send it to your essay partner 🤍
