## Caption

english essays get marked on the first line of each paragraph, not the quote you pasted.

1. make the opening sentence the argument. if it restates the topic, you already lost the A.
2. drop the evidence, then write what it does. they mark your sentence, not shakespeare's.
3. let chatgpt draft the first pass. don't submit that. your job starts after the page exists.

run that AI draft through AntiGPT before you submit. it passes the human threshold on turnitin and AI detectors. link in bio.

save this before your next essay.
