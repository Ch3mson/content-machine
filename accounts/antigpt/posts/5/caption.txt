TITLE
3 GPT prompts for stronger essays

DESCRIPTION
give GPT a specific job when you edit your essay ✍️

paste your thesis and ask it to make the argument more specific. share your evidence notes to get help arranging your body paragraphs, or paste your conclusion + thesis and ask how they could connect.

include your essay question so GPT has something to work towards. pick the suggestions that fit your argument, then try AntiGPT for more natural wording.

save these prompts for the next time you’re stuck on a draft 📚
