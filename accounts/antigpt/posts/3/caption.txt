3 ways to write your next essay faster

DESCRIPTION
3 ways to write your next essay faster 📚

start by asking AI for papers related to your question, then open the links and check which ones you can use. paste your essay question + marking rubric into GPT for an outline, and give it your source notes to draft each section.

once you have a draft, try AntiGPT to make the wording sound more human. read it through and check that it still says what you mean.

save this for your next deadline 🤍
