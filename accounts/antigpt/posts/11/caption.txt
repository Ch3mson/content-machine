TITLE
3 GPT prompts for better essay paragraphs

DESCRIPTION
use GPT for the part of a paragraph you are stuck on. you can get more useful suggestions by naming the exact problem.

for an unclear opening, try: "what is the main claim in this paragraph? suggest a topic sentence that states it." for a quote that feels dropped in, paste your claim and source notes and ask how the evidence supports the argument. check that explanation against the source.

if two paragraphs feel disconnected, paste both and ask GPT to explain their relationship before suggesting a transition. choose wording that reflects the connection you mean.
