TITLE
3 GPT shortcuts for writing your introduction

DESCRIPTION
you can draft an introduction once you know what the essay argues. give GPT your outline or body paragraphs and start with the thesis.

ask: "state the central argument of this essay in one specific sentence." check whether that sentence says what you mean. then ask which background details a reader needs to understand the argument. choose the details that matter for your question.

paste your thesis and chosen context together and ask for a short introduction within your word budget. compare it with the body of the essay so the opening promises the discussion you actually deliver.

save this for the introduction you have been avoiding. AntiGPT at antigpt.me can help with the final wording: it rewrites AI text to sound human and helps bypass AI detectors.
