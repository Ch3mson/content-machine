## Posting title

3 ways to use your rubric with GPT

## Posting description

your marking rubric is useful before you finish the essay. paste it into GPT alongside the assignment question and use it to plan your draft.

ask: "explain each criterion in plain language and show what i should include to address it." if analysis carries more marks than description, ask for paragraph goals that leave room to explain your evidence.

once you have a draft, paste it back with the rubric. ask GPT to point to a specific paragraph for each criterion, then flag anything your draft has not addressed. treat the response as revision suggestions and check it against the actual instructions.

keep this for your next assignment. AntiGPT at antigpt.me can help with the wording after your argument is in place: it rewrites AI text to sound human and helps bypass AI detectors.
