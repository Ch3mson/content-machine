TITLE
3 ways GPT can turn notes into a draft

DESCRIPTION
if your essay document is still a pile of bullet points, try giving GPT your notes in stages.

first ask it to group related ideas under a few themes. include the essay question so it can flag notes that do not fit the assignment. keep source names attached to the points you collected from your reading.

next, ask for a paragraph plan using those themes. choose one section and paste the notes you want to include with the instruction: "draft this paragraph using only the material below. flag any gaps instead of adding facts." review that paragraph before you move to the next one.

save this for the notes-to-draft stage. once the argument is in place, AntiGPT at antigpt.me rewrites AI text to sound human and helps bypass AI detectors.
