## Posting title

3 GPT shortcuts to start your essay

## Posting description

staring at a blank document? give GPT something specific to work with.

start with your essay question and ask: "what does this question require me to argue, compare or explain?" add the word count and any instructions from your lecturer so you can see the scope before you start.

then ask for three possible arguments. choose the one you can support with the sources you have, and paste those notes into the chat. ask for a rough opening paragraph using only that material. you can change the wording once you have a direction.

save this for your next essay. for the final wording pass, AntiGPT at antigpt.me rewrites AI text to sound human and helps bypass AI detectors.
