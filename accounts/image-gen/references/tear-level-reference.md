## Visual Benchmark: Why This Level Works

1. **Tear Level**:
   - Subtle, natural sheen and moisture along the lower eyelid and waterline.
   - A single delicate tear track running down the cheek.
   - No heavy artificial streaks, no mascara running, and no exaggerated movie-style weeping.

2. **Eye Appearance**:
   - Glassy, watery, and reflective.
   - Natural clear eyes: **NO severe bloodshot veins, NO heavily swollen/puffy eyelids**.
   - Subtle flush around the nose and upper cheekbones that looks like quiet crying or holding back tears, rather than allergic reaction or sickness.

3. **Gaze & Pose**:
   - Head/body oriented toward the mirror (the camera).
   - Eyes cast slightly downward or off-axis, avoiding direct aggressive eye contact with the lens.
   - Chin or cheek resting naturally in palm/fingers, conveying fatigue, defeat, and emotional overwhelm.

## Prompt Guidance for Replicating This Level

When drafting future prompts, use this phrasing:

```text
She has been quietly crying: glassy, watery eyes with subtle glistening moisture, a single delicate tear track on her cheek, quiet emotional heartbreak and defeated vulnerable expression. She faces toward the mirror/camera but her eyes gaze slightly off to the side, looking away from the camera lens. Natural clear eyes without heavy bloodshot redness, without extreme puffiness, and without swollen eyelids.
```
