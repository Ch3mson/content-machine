## Format Families

### A. Crying-girl single image (the one that worked)

Same visual every time: AI-looking blonde girl crying in a dorm/bedroom, mirror selfie or bed selfie, tissues, hoodie, default TikTok white text with black outline.

Three copy templates, with small swaps:

1. **Fast-food rejection + overqualified proof**
   `Day was going [well/fine] until I got the email saying I didn't get the [Wendy's/McDonalds/KFC] job... with a [3.8/3.9/4.0] GPA and a masters degree... This market is unreal 😭`
2. **Who is actually getting hired**
   `I get that the job market is awful right now, people with degrees can't even land entry level roles... so who [is / the f is / tf is] actually getting hired? Bc I haven't seen a single person 😭`
3. **Duration / volume despair**
   `Nearly 18 months out of college, 1600+ job apps and still nothing... am I actually unemployable or is this job market broken 😭`

Optional second text block (soft site CTA):

`any tools besides howtogetajobasap.com I can use to mass apply to jobs??`

or

`Friend at Google told me abt howtogetajobasap.com so I'm hopeful`

### B. Slideshows (much weaker)

Lifestyle / "clean girl" / desk-aesthetic carousels with boxed career tips. These look like normal advice slideshows and did 2k–5k plays.

## Reuse Notes

- Do not copy the Wendy's / McDonald's / KFC + GPA + masters wording.
- Do not use `howtogetajobasap.com` or invent a similar "mass apply" product claim.
- The reusable move is: one still of a girl mid-cry + one paragraph of private humiliation + a status contradiction (overqualified / no one is hiring / I did everything).
- Captions are throwaway hashtags. The on-image paragraph is the whole post.
- Photo titles are the real hook language: `why is it impossible to get a job`, `is anyone getting hired?`, `how do I get a job??`
- Slideshows on this account are a different, weaker format. If testing a single image for AntiGPT, start from family A, not B.
