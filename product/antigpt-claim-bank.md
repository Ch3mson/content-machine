file. Use approved claims freely. Qualify caution claims. Never use blocked
claims. The CTA line (post caption or short on-image close) is the only place
the product appears, so most claim use happens there.

## Caution Claims

Use with care. Qualify or soften.

| Claim | Risk | How to use safely |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100% undetectable / never gets flagged | Absolute outcome; detectors change | Say "bypasses" or "built to pass", not "100%" or "never" |
| Specific pass rates (e.g. "1% detection") | Only a marketing graphic on site; unverified | Skip unless the user supplies a real test result |
| "1,000,000+ writers" / "250,000+ students" | Site figures conflict with each other | Do not use until the user confirms a number |
| Named-school testimonials | Marketing copy, not verified users | Do not quote as real reviews |
| "Saves hours on essays" | Outcome varies | Frame as what students use it for, not a guarantee |
| Comparisons to named competitors (QuillBot, Undetectable AI, StealthGPT) | Competitor-mention risk | Prefer category comparisons in slides; blog handles head-to-heads |
| Back to School 45% off | Expires September 30, 2026 | Only while live; date-stamp any post that mentions it |
| Free trial | It is 100 words with card on file, then charged | Say "free AI checker" instead; do not imply a no-card free trial |

## Blocked Claims

Never use these in any content.

| Claim | Why it is blocked |
| --- | --- |
| Fake testimonials, fake screenshots, fabricated scores | Legal liability; trust destruction |
| Guaranteed grades or academic outcomes | Outcome guarantee |
| "AntiGPT writes your essay for you" as the humanizer claim | Misdescribes the humanizer; only Stealth Writer drafts, and only if the post is about that feature |
| Any reference to antigpt.app, its LinkedIn, Product Hunt, or user counts | Different company |
| Claims about detectors AntiGPT has not been tested against | Unverified |
| Implying university endorsement ("Harvard uses AntiGPT") | Logos are user-institution signals, not partnerships |

## Risk Note (for the record)

Direct "bypass Turnitin" copy is the user's explicit choice and matches the
product's own marketing. It carries platform-moderation and academic-integrity
optics risk on Instagram and TikTok. Keep it in the CTA line, keep the hook line
about the student moment, and do not stack multiple bypass claims in one post.
